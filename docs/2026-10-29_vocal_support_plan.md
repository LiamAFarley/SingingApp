# Vocal support and stability — approach plan

Date: 2026-10-29. For Liam. This is the approach, not the routine.

## What failed before this

Tables of pitches and reps with one-line sensation notes. Straw protocols invented before the source file was opened. Vague cues such as deliberately light and chest vibration with no mechanism. A misnamed ping-pong block. App blocks proposed for airflow, pressure, vowel and loudness, which the microphone cannot measure. Random-tone masks proposed after an explicit ban. One-sentence patches applied to structural gaps.

## What good looks like

Every exercise carries nine fields filled from a stated passage: purpose, setup, execution, sensation, focus, pitfall plus correction, move-on criterion, citation with chapter or exercise number and line or page range, app mapping. Anything missing a field stays out until its passage is extracted. No exercise enters from memory.

## Sources to mine chapter by chapter

- Ragan, A Systematic Approach to Voice (2020). Chapters 3 and 4 carry the load: sustained unvoiced consonants 10 to 20 s, rhythmic patterns at 60 bpm, alternating unvoiced and voiced pairs at one phoneme per beat, Flow-ball baseline and voiceless staccato and voiceless messa di voce, flownation, breath depletion conditioning, /um/ patterns, lip trill arpeggios, chromatic lip trills, staccato 4.10 with /ba/ and silent /h/, SOVT pressure ladder, onsets.
- LeBorgne and Rosenberg, The Vocal Athlete, 3rd edition (2024). SOVT and inertance mechanism, phonation threshold pressure, laryngeal chapters, tongue tension and laryngeal massage passages.
- Malde, What Every Singer Needs to Know about the Body (2020). Body mapping, balance, head separate from jaw, neck release as the balance signal, tongue tension remedy, vibrato cause list and entry point.
- Bickel, Vocal Technique: A Physiologic Approach (2016). Chapter 4 breath management: hissing S, counting, card buzzing with 3x5 card, sustaining lower rib expansion, lifted sternum.
- Vocal Timbre and Feminization Techniques (docx). Sections 1 to 5: six straw protocols, weight thinning, NEE and twang, heat from fire phonetics, sibilant placement, prosody.
- Vocal Pedagogy and Spectrogram Analysis (rtf) plus the spectrogram and tutorial reference notes. Checked for anything on support and stability; kept only if it adds what the five above lack.
- Sadolin Complete Vocal Technique PDFs are noted as unextractable (215 MB yielding about 11 kB of text, and a 260-byte layer). Any reader assigned them reports that state rather than inventing content.

## Method

One reader per book. Each reads chapter by chapter and returns only support, evenness, stability, tension release, breathing, onset, SOVT, and registration material. Each finding states the passage location, the setup, the execution, the sensation cue as written, the pitfall and correction as written, and what it would become in the routine. Findings without a location are discarded.
